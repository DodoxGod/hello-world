# Ideas: better or worse metal of the same alloy (saved for later)

Andy, 2026-10-01: "son buenas, guardarlas para verlo después". Nothing has been decided yet.

1. **Ingot purity:** impure, common, pure or perfect. It changes the durability and damage of the piece by −10 % to +10 %, and shows in the tooltip.
2. **Mix proportion:** each alloy has an ideal mix, and straying from it lowers the purity.
3. **Melting temperature:** the right heat gives pure metal; too cold leaves slag, too hot burns it. This uses the existing heat system.
4. **Slag and refining:** the strainer or crucible raises the purity one grade, at the cost of some metal.
5. **Flux and additives:** e.g. bone meal, charcoal, blaze powder, quartz. They raise the purity or add a small bonus.
6. **Quenching the ingot:** water makes it harder but more brittle; oil leaves it balanced.
7. **Smith level and mastery:** they raise the chance of pure ingots.
8. **Cracked ingots:** pieces with less durability. They sell cheap, or go into kits.
9. **Master ingot:** very rare; only with a perfect mix, temperature and additive. It adds an extra trait to the piece.
10. **Mixed grades:** the main part decides the quality of the piece.

Suggested start: 1 + 3 + 4, then 5 or 9.
